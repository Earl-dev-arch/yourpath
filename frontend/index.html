BEFORE YOU CONTINUE

Cookies & privacy

YourPath uses one essential, HttpOnly session cookie to keep your profile, questionnaire and reflection connected while you use the site. It lasts up to eight hours. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

What is stored: your student details, password hash, answers, reflection and feedback in this server's memory.

How long: until you erase your account, the session expires, or the server restarts. Restarting this prototype removes all accounts.

Read the privacy policy

Purpose. Your information is used only to connect your profile to your questionnaire, interest reflection, career research, roadmap and any feedback you submit.

Cookies. The essential session cookie is HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict. It is not used for cross-site tracking. It is required for sign-up, sign-in and saving your work.

Prototype limits. Data and salted password hashes are held in process memory, not a durable or encrypted database. Anyone with access to this development machine or server may be able to access information. Do not enter real student data or a password you use elsewhere.

Your choices. You can leave before creating an account, log out, or erase your account and associated answers from the running server. No data is sent to an AI provider.

Before real use. A deployed service for students needs HTTPS, durable encryption, access controls, a retention schedule, verified guardian-consent processes and legal/privacy review.

Account features need the YourPath app server. Start it, then open http://localhost:5080.

For the prototype only. Do not enter sensitive personal information or reuse an important password.
